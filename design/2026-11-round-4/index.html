Search and agenda

  • S3 on-demand search: click the search icon or press Ctrl+F. The field immediately receives focus.
  • A query stays visible while navigating results. An empty search returns to “Next events” after leaving its controls.
  • Preferences → Appearance → Upcoming events: A1 event cards, A2 day groups, or A3 per-day timeline. A3 is the default.
  • Search retains source-colored cards and native series folding in every agenda style.
  • Day headings align with the outer edge; timeline rails stop at day boundaries.

Calendar list

  • Read-only calendars show a small lock and an explanatory tooltip.
  • Drag a calendar’s name or color square to reorder. A line previews the insertion position.
  • The order is saved per profile and survives restarting. Reordering preserves the calendar used for creating events.
  • The chosen calendar retains its bold underline, without an extra transient focus border.
  • The applied header keeps its existing layout while the alternatives below are reviewed.

Navigation & controls · N1–N6

May and September are shown together using the same reserved label width. The Next button never moves when the month changes. Compare the visible empty space after May with the alternative that puts all navigation after the range.

How I would choose: N1/N3 retain the preferred H3/H4/H7 geometry; N3 reduces the title and controls together. Their short-month gap is the visible cost of keeping Next fixed. N5 removes that gap and keeps navigation together, with range-before-buttons order. The underlined styles are quieter, but need the rule below the toolbar for separation. N2/N4/N6 give a fairer filled comparison: selection reaches the group outline, with no white moat, inner border or unequal inset.

Sidebar alignment · L1–L3

These alternatives answer whether source controls, headings, search and event frames need one shared edge. None adds hierarchy through arbitrary heading indentation. Compare the resting A3 agenda and an expanded search result, since a timeline cannot define search structure.

Tradeoff: L1 is closest to the applied A3. Its date headings and source squares align, while event frames start after the clock column. L2 gives every frame a common outer edge but puts more line work inside cards. L3 treats the inset as a real utility lane, with the cost of narrower search and title space. I would compare L1 and L2 first; L3 is useful if a common text edge matters more than horizontal room.
