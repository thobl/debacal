Editor and picker states

Appearance

The primary event stays editable

Start and End refer to the primary event, including after the series has been created. The preview has its own role: arranging and navigating the additional occurrences.

  • Start, End and Duration are grouped together.
  • The primary occurrence has a distinct indicator.
  • Month paging and occurrence navigation are separate.

One shared picker

The Start and End controls open the same date/range picker. Add occurrence reuses it, with the primary event’s clock and duration as the initial values.

Three related jobs, with their controls together

Define the primary event

Use the written Start and End fields or their picker buttons. Duration and its quick choices sit alongside them; changing duration adjusts End.

Arrange the occurrences

Click preview dates to add or remove extra days. Use Add occurrence when another date/time needs its own choice.

Inspect the series

Navigate to the primary, previous or next occurrence. These controls follow events; the month arrows change the preview’s page.
